    About

    NBack Trainer follows the dual N-back task from Jaeggi et al. (2008): a 3×3 grid, eight spoken letters, 20 + N items per session, and a level that adapts to you. Triple mode adds a color stream.

    Keyboard: A position, F color, L sound, Space pause, Esc end.

    This phone needs an update first

    NBack uses display features that this device's built-in browser engine is too old for, so the screen may look broken.

    Open the Play Store, update Android System WebView and Chrome, then reopen NBack.

    How to play

    1. 2

      Every few seconds a square lights up and a letter is spoken.

    2. KSK

      Press Sound when the letter is the same as N steps ago. Press Position when the square is the same. Both can match at once.

    3. PositionSound

      Do nothing when there is no match. Make fewer than 3 mistakes on each button and N goes up next time. More than 5 on either one and it goes down.

    On a foldable, half-open the phone and set it down: the grid sits on the top screen and the buttons on the bottom.
