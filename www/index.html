    About

    NBack Trainer follows the dual N-back task from Jaeggi et al. (2008): a 3×3 grid, eight spoken letters, 20 + N trials per block, and an adaptive level. Triple mode adds a colour stream.

    Keyboard: A position, F colour, L sound, Space pause, Esc end.

    How to play

    1. 2

      Every few seconds a square lights up and a letter is spoken.

    2. KSK

      Press Sound when the letter is the same as N steps ago. Press Position when the square is the same. Both can match at once.

    3. PositionSound

      Do nothing when there is no match. Fewer than 3 errors raises N next time, more than 5 lowers it.

    On a foldable, half-open the phone and set it down: the grid sits on the top screen and the buttons on the bottom.
